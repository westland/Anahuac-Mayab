# J. Christopher Westland | Anáhuac Mayab – Fulbright Garcia-Robles

This repository serves as a centralized hub for shared materials, lecture notes, and student presentations created during my tenure as a **Fulbright Garcia-Robles Scholar** at **Universidad Anáhuac Mayab** in Mérida, Mexico.

## 🎓 About the Author

**J. Christopher Westland** is a Professor in the Department of Information and Decision Sciences at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). He is a leading expert in the fields of Financial Technology (FinTech), Information Economics, and Decision Sciences.

### Professional Background
*   **Current Primary Appointment:** Professor, UIC Business (Information and Decision Sciences).
*   **Education:** PhD in Computers and Information Systems from the **University of Michigan**.
*   **Research Focus:** His work investigates the intersection of technology and economics, specifically:
    *   Financial Dynamics and Complexity.
    *   Audit Analytics and Blockchain.
    *   Valuation of Information Technology.
    *   Global Innovation Management.
*   **Editorial Leadership:** He serves as the Editor-in-Chief of the journal *Electronic Commerce Research* (Springer) and has served on numerous editorial boards for journals such as *Management Science* and *MIS Quarterly*.
*   **Authorship:** Author of several foundational books, including *Financial Dynamics: A Systems Approach*, *Audit Analytics*, and *Global Innovation Management*.
*   **Global Impact:** He has held numerous visiting professorships and faculty positions worldwide, notably at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

## 🇲🇽 Fulbright Garcia-Robles Scholarship
I am currently a **Fulbright Garcia-Robles Scholar**, a prestigious grant awarded by the **U.S. Department of State** and the **United States-Mexico Commission for Educational and Cultural Exchange (COMEXUS)**. 

Through this scholarship, I am hosted by **Universidad Anáhuac Mayab** (Mérida, Yucatán), where I collaborate with faculty and students to foster cross-border academic excellence, specifically focusing on the evolution of technology and business systems in the Mexican context.

## 📂 Repository Contents
This repository is specifically curated for **Anáhuac Mayab students**. It contains:
